I am a health system modeller and epidemiologist with 6 years of experience bridging mathematical modelling, health economics, and data science to drive evidence-based policy. Passionate about real-world impact and equitable public health, recognising patient, system, and resource barriers. I enjoy programming alongside data analysis and visualisation. I strongly value cross-disciplinary collaboration and connecting with staff across governmental and intergovernmental institutions to reach actionable impact.

## Research interests
My primary research interest is in public health and interventions to strengthen health systems, improve care delivery, and achieve equitable health outcomes. I am also enthusiastic about broader sectors that indirectly shape public health, including education, social care, urban planning, agriculture, food security, and climate change. Main topics I am passionate about:

* **Epidemiological modelling:** Individual-based models (IBMs) and stochastic network simulations in Python, Julia, and R. I enjoy learning about new diseases, their epidemiology, and modelling real-world health system challenges. I apply established domain knowledge (strong: acute malnutrition, bat-driven rabies, zoonotic and non-zoonotic schistosomiasis; competent: HIV, TB, malaria, ALRI) and am always eager to expand into new disease areas.

* **Disease spillover & One Health:** Zoonotic diseases, cross-species spillover dynamics, and geospatial risk mapping.

* **Cost-effectiveness evaluation:** Economic evaluations of health policy strategies and public health interventions. Quantifying health gains (DALYs averted) by integrating dynamic epidemiological models with cost estimates, determining cost-effectiveness thresholds, utilising cost-effectiveness frontiers, applying discounting to outcomes and costs, and calculating INHB & INMB metrics. Motivated to incorporate equity and environmental impact into intervention evaluations.

* **Emergency preparedness & resilience:** Keen to explore barriers and facilitators to healthcare access and health outcomes during emergencies such as pandemics, environmental shocks, conflicts, and economic crises. Interested in learning more about response strategy design and evaluation, emergency detection improvement, and health system resilience frameworks.

* **Biosecurity:** Highly interested in expanding my focus into biosafety protocols, early-warning detection systems for novel pathogens, dual-use research oversight, and policy frameworks for mitigating biological risks.

* **Geospatial modelling & analysis:** Enticed by incorporating spatial data to account for geographic variation in transmission dynamics, healthcare access, and environmental exposures, improving predictions and targeting interventions effectively.
